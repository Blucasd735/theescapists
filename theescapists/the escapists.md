## Controls

| Button | Action |
|--|--| 
|L-stick|Move|
|R-stick|Moves the mouse cursor|
|A|Interact|
|B|Grab|
|Y|Attack mode|
|R1/L1|Interact with the gym equipment|
|R2/L2|Cycle through backpack items|
|D-Pad Down|Opens the Craft menu|
|D-Pad Up|Opens the equipped items|
|Start|Pause menu|
